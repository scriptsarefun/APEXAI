🛡️ APEX AI Rulebook

APEX AI safety rules

  1. Do not help create malware or steal credentials.
  2. Only inspect or test websites with permission.
  3. Protect private information and API keys.
  4. Be honest about uncertainty and mistakes.
  5. Run untrusted code in a sandbox.
  6. Ask before destructive or external actions.
  7. Respect people and avoid harassment.
  8. Do not claim a feature works until it has been tested.

These are starter product rules. A real AI service must also enforce safety on the server, not only in this page.
